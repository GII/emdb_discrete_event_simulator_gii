OnRobot 2FG7 for MuJoCo (2fg7.xml)

Source: https://github.com/onrobot/onrobot-ros2, package onrobot_2fg7
  commit 1b24536e4ae58b127025a4336fd1f06109c54801 (2026-10-19, "Fix RG body mounts and prepare ROS suite 1.3.1 (#10)"),

Configuration: standard fingertips, fingertip_material silicone, fingertip_orientation outwards (the lab gripper),
  mounted directly on the UR5e flange.

Files
- assets/finger_base_link.stl, assets/silicone_fingertip_link.stl: onrobot_2fg7/meshes/*.STL, unchanged (only the
  extension lowercased).
- assets/base_link.obj: onrobot_2fg7/meshes/base_link.STL (204,155 triangles) converted to OBJ without loss (the
  same triangles, shared vertices merged), because MuJoCo's STL reader accepts at most 200,000 faces.
  The official .dae visuals are Git LFS objects and were not used; the STLs are what the official "realmesh"
  description uses for collision, in the same frames.
- urdf/onrobot_2fg7_macro.xacro: unchanged, for reference.
- urdf/onrobot_2fg7_silicone_outwards.urdf: generated from it with
    xacro <wrapper calling xacro:onrobot_2fg7 include_ros2_control="false"> \
      fingertip_material:=silicone fingertip_orientation:=outwards mesh_path:=package://onrobot_2fg7/meshes
- 2fg7.xml: written from that URDF: body frames, finger joints and stroke, masses, inertias and the collision boxes
  (body box, silicone gripping plates, fingertip mounting blocks) are the URDF's. Differences:
  1. The finger joint value is the distance from the centre to the finger's pad face (ref 16.5 mm), so the URDF's
     stroke 0..19 mm is 16.5..35.5 mm and the tendon right + left is the aperture between the pads.
  2. Aperture 33-71 mm, not the datasheet's 35-73 mm: the datasheet range is for the aluminium fingertips, and the
     silicone layer is 1 mm thick on each face (datasheet: "silicone fingertips add 1 mm in each direction"; the
     official silicone collision plates are 1 mm thicker per side than the aluminium ones).
  3. Fingertip inertia: the plain URDF's orientation. The xacro turns the fingertip's inertial frame by pi about x,
     which flips the sign of its products of inertia; the plain URDF's sign fits the L-shaped fingertip.
     Negligible either way (33 g).
  4. Finger base inertial: the xacro gives one finger a centre-of-mass offset and the other none; both get the offset
     (they are the same part).
  5. MuJoCo-only parts, mine (the URDF has nothing for MuJoCo): the aperture servo (kp 10000 N/m, kv 100 N s/m,
     force limit = grip force), the finger coupling (joint equality), armature 0.5 and damping 2 on the finger
     joints, and the pad contact (friction 1 / 0.005 / 0.0001, condim 4, solref 0.004 1, priority 1). The URDF's
     Gazebo friction for silicone is 0.4, which OnRobot's README calls a simulation input to adjust, not a
     measured value.

Datasheet (OnRobot 2FG7 datasheet v2.0, https://onrobot.com/storage/datasheets/2fg7/datasheet_2fg7_v2.0_en.pdf):
  stroke 38 mm; external grip 1-39 mm (fingers inwards) / 35-73 mm (outwards); force 20-140 N, +-5 N; speed
  16-450 mm/s; gripping time ~200 ms (4 mm stroke, 80 N, incl. brake), ~300 ms (38 mm, 80 N); repeatability
  +-0.1 mm; 1.1 kg; force sensor in one finger; holds the workpiece if power is lost.
